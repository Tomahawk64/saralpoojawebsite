1. Introduction

Welcome to Saral Pooja. At Saral Pooja, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share, and protect your information when you use our mobile application, website, or any services provided through our platform. We believe that you should always know what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used.

By creating an account, accessing our platform, booking a religious service, making a payment, or using any feature of Saral Pooja, you agree to the collection and use of your information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this Policy, you should stop using our Services.

Saral Pooja is a digital platform that connects users with verified pandits and religious service providers for pujas, rituals, temple services, spiritual consultations, and other devotional services. To provide these services efficiently and securely, we need to collect certain information from our users. We collect only the information that is necessary to operate our platform, fulfil bookings, improve our services, comply with legal requirements, and provide customer support.

Your trust is important to us. We do not sell your personal information to anyone. We collect information only for legitimate business purposes and take reasonable security measures to protect it from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

This Privacy Policy applies to all users of Saral Pooja, including visitors, registered users, customers, pandits, and anyone who interacts with our platform. It applies whether you use our Android application, iOS application, website, customer support channels, WhatsApp support, email, or any other official communication channel operated by Saral Pooja.

From time to time, we may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our services, legal requirements, or technology. Whenever we make significant changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date shown at the beginning of this document. Continued use of the platform after such updates means you accept the revised Privacy Policy.

Protecting your personal information is one of our priorities. We are committed to collecting only the information that is necessary to provide our services, keeping that information secure, and using it responsibly.

We do not collect unnecessary personal information, and we do not use your information for purposes unrelated to the services we provide unless you have given your consent or we are required to do so by law.

Whenever possible, we use secure technologies and industry-standard practices to safeguard your information. While no online service can guarantee absolute security, we continuously work to improve our systems and processes to reduce risks and protect your data.

5. Information We Collect

To provide our Services efficiently and securely, Saral Pooja collects certain information from users. The type of information we collect depends on how you use our Platform and the services you request. We collect only the information that is reasonably necessary to operate our business, process bookings, provide customer support, comply with legal obligations, and improve the overall user experience.

Personal Information

When you register an account or use our Services, we may collect personal information such as your full name, mobile number, email address, profile photograph (if you choose to upload one), date of birth (where voluntarily provided), gender (optional), preferred language, and other profile details that you choose to share with us. Some information is required to create and manage your account, while other information is optional and is collected only if you decide to provide it.

Contact Information

To communicate with you regarding your bookings, payments, account activity, and customer support requests, we may collect your mobile number and email address. We may use these details to send one-time passwords (OTPs), booking confirmations, reminders, invoices, service updates, security notifications, and responses to your support requests.

Address Information

If you book a home puja, request delivery of prasad or religious items, or use any service that requires a physical location, we may collect your residential or delivery address. This may include your house or apartment number, street name, landmark, city, district, state, postal code, and country. This information is used only to provide the requested service and maintain accurate transaction records.

Religious Information

Certain services available on Saral Pooja may require information that is traditionally used in Hindu religious practices. Depending on the service you choose, you may voluntarily provide details such as your gotra, rashi, nakshatra, date of birth, time of birth, place of birth, preferred deity, names of family members participating in a ceremony, or other relevant information. This information is requested only when it is necessary to perform the religious service you have selected. You are not required to provide such information unless it is relevant to your booking.

Booking Information

Whenever you book a puja, pandit, temple service, consultation, or any other service through our Platform, we collect information related to that booking. This may include the type of service requested, preferred date and time, venue, special instructions, number of participants, booking status, booking history, and communication records associated with that booking. We use this information to coordinate with service providers, manage your booking, send updates, and provide customer support.

Payment Information

When you make a payment through Saral Pooja, your transaction is processed securely through authorized payment gateways. We may receive information such as transaction ID, payment status, payment method, invoice details, refund status, booking amount, taxes, and payment timestamps. We do not store your complete debit card number, credit card number, CVV, UPI PIN, internet banking password, or any other sensitive payment authentication credentials. These details are handled directly by the payment service provider in accordance with its own security standards and privacy practices.

Customer Support Information

If you contact our customer support team by phone, email, WhatsApp, live chat, or any other communication channel, we may collect the information you choose to share with us. This may include your queries, complaints, feedback, suggestions, documents, images, or other materials required to resolve your request. We may also maintain records of our communications with you to improve service quality, resolve disputes, and comply with legal obligations.

Device Information

When you use the Platform, certain technical information is collected automatically from your device. This may include your device model, operating system, browser type, application version, device identifiers, IP address, language settings, time zone, network information, crash reports, and other technical information required to ensure the proper functioning and security of our Platform.

Usage Information

We may collect information about how you interact with the Platform. This includes pages viewed, features used, searches performed, booking history, time spent on different sections of the application, click patterns, navigation behavior, and general usage statistics. This information helps us understand how users interact with our Services so that we can improve performance, introduce new features, and provide a better user experience.

Location Information

If you allow location access on your device, Saral Pooja may collect your approximate or precise location. This information helps us identify nearby temples or verified pandits, determine service availability, provide home services, improve search results, and offer location-based features. You can disable location access at any time through your device settings, although doing so may affect certain features of the Platform.

Camera, Microphone, and Photo Gallery

Some features of the Platform may request permission to access your device's camera, microphone, or photo gallery. These permissions are requested only when necessary for a specific feature, such as uploading a profile picture, sharing documents with customer support, or submitting images related to a booking or complaint. We access these features only after obtaining your permission, and you may withdraw such permissions at any time through your device settings.

Information from Third Parties

In certain situations, we may receive information about you from trusted third parties. This may include payment confirmation from payment gateways, delivery updates from logistics partners, verification information from service providers, or technical reports from analytics providers. We process such information only for lawful purposes related to the Services offered by Saral Pooja and in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

Information You Choose Not to Provide

Providing certain information is optional. However, if you choose not to provide information that is necessary for a particular service, we may be unable to create your account, process your booking, complete your transaction, verify your identity where required, or provide the requested service. We will always try to inform you when specific information is necessary and explain why it is required.

Accuracy of Information

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you provide to Saral Pooja is accurate, complete, and up to date. If your contact details or other important information changes, you should update your account or notify us so that we can continue to provide our Services effectively. Saral Pooja is not responsible for any issues, delays, or losses resulting from incorrect or outdated information provided by you.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website and certain features of our Platform may use cookies and similar technologies to improve functionality, enhance user experience, understand user behavior, and maintain security.

Cookies are small text files that are stored on your device when you visit a website. They help remember your preferences, recognize returning users, improve website performance, and provide a smoother browsing experience.

Saral Pooja may use cookies for purposes such as keeping you signed in, remembering your language preferences, maintaining session security, measuring website traffic, understanding user interactions, and improving the performance of our Services.

In addition to cookies, we may use similar technologies such as web beacons, pixels, software development kits (SDKs), or local storage where appropriate. These technologies help us analyze application performance, measure the effectiveness of communications, and improve our Platform.

Most web browsers allow you to manage or disable cookies through browser settings. Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of certain features and may prevent some parts of the Platform from working properly.

We do not use cookies to collect sensitive personal information without your knowledge. Any use of cookies is intended to support the operation, security, and improvement of our Services.
