1. Introduction

At Saral Pooja, we respect your right to control your personal information and your account. This Account Deletion Policy explains how you can request the deletion of your Saral Pooja account, what happens after your request is received, what information will be deleted, what information may be retained, and how we process deletion requests in accordance with applicable laws.

We understand that there may be various reasons why a user wishes to stop using our Services. Whether you are switching to another platform, no longer require our services, or simply wish to remove your account, we aim to make the account deletion process transparent, secure, and straightforward.

This Policy should be read together with our Privacy Policy, Terms and Conditions, and Refund Policy, as these documents explain how your information is collected, used, retained, and protected.

2. Who Can Request Account Deletion

Any registered user of the Saral Pooja Platform may request the deletion of their account.

To protect user privacy and prevent unauthorized account removal, deletion requests may only be submitted by the account holder or an authorized representative where permitted by applicable law. Saral Pooja may require reasonable verification of identity before processing any deletion request.

If we are unable to verify that the request has been made by the rightful account owner, we may refuse or delay the request until sufficient verification has been completed.

3. Ways to Delete Your Account

Saral Pooja may provide one or more methods for users to delete their accounts.

If the account deletion feature is available within the mobile application or website, you may submit your request through the account settings by following the instructions displayed on the Platform.

If the self-service option is not available or you require assistance, you may contact our customer support team using the contact details provided on the Platform. Our team may request limited information to verify your identity before processing the request.

Users should not share passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, or other confidential authentication information while requesting account deletion.

4. Verification Process

Before permanently deleting an account, Saral Pooja may verify the identity of the person making the request.

Verification helps prevent unauthorized persons from deleting someone else's account and protects users against fraud or misuse.

Depending on the circumstances, verification may include confirmation through the registered mobile number, registered email address, one-time password (OTP), account information, or any other reasonable verification method considered appropriate.

If verification cannot be completed successfully, Saral Pooja may decline the request until the identity of the requester has been established.

5. What Happens After You Submit a Request

Once your deletion request has been successfully verified, Saral Pooja will begin processing your request within a reasonable period.

During this period, your account may be temporarily restricted while verification and internal processing are completed.

After the deletion process is completed, you will generally lose access to your account, profile, booking history, saved preferences, loyalty benefits, and other account-related features associated with that account.

Once an account has been permanently deleted, it generally cannot be restored. If you wish to use Saral Pooja again in the future, you may need to register a new account and complete the registration process again.

6. Information That Will Be Deleted

Subject to applicable law and the exceptions described in this Policy, Saral Pooja will make reasonable efforts to permanently delete or anonymize personal information that is no longer required.

This may include your profile information, saved addresses, account preferences, uploaded profile photographs, saved payment preferences (where applicable), communication preferences, and other personal information associated with your account.

Where technically feasible, such information will be removed from our active systems once the deletion process has been completed.

Please note that certain copies of information may continue to exist in encrypted backups or disaster recovery systems for a limited period until those backups are overwritten in the ordinary course of business. Such information will not be used for any purpose except system recovery, legal compliance, or security where required.

7. Information That May Be Retained

Deleting your account does not necessarily mean that every record associated with your account can be immediately removed.

Saral Pooja may retain certain information where required by applicable law or where retention is reasonably necessary for legitimate business purposes. This may include transaction records, invoices, payment records, tax information, completed booking records, fraud prevention data, dispute-related information, audit records, customer support records, legal compliance records, and information required to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

Any retained information will continue to be protected in accordance with our Privacy Policy and applicable laws and will not be retained for longer than reasonably necessary.

8. Pending Bookings and Ongoing Services

Requesting the deletion of your account does not automatically cancel any confirmed bookings or services that have already been scheduled. If you have an active booking at the time you request account deletion, the booking may continue to be governed by our Terms and Conditions and Refund Policy.

Depending on the stage of the booking, Saral Pooja may process the service before completing the account deletion request or may ask you to cancel or complete the booking first. This helps avoid disruptions, payment disputes, or inconvenience to the assigned pandit or service provider.

If you have requested a refund or raised a complaint regarding an existing booking, we may postpone the completion of your account deletion request until the matter has been resolved.

9. Refunds After Account Deletion

Deleting your account does not affect your eligibility for a refund if you are otherwise entitled to one under our Refund Policy.

Where a refund has been approved before or after your deletion request, Saral Pooja may process the refund using the original payment method or another legally permitted method, even if your account has already been deleted.

Users are encouraged to ensure that their registered contact information remains accessible until all pending refunds, support requests, or disputes have been fully resolved.

10. Re-Registration After Account Deletion

If you choose to use Saral Pooja again after your account has been permanently deleted, you may create a new account using the registration process available at that time.

A newly created account will generally be treated as a separate account. Previous booking history, loyalty benefits, promotional rewards, saved preferences, profile information, and other deleted account data may not be restored unless retention is required by law or technically possible.

Saral Pooja reserves the right to refuse registration if a previous account was terminated due to fraud, misuse of the Platform, repeated violations of our policies, or any unlawful activity.

11. Your Responsibilities Before Deleting Your Account

Before requesting account deletion, you should review your account and ensure that there are no pending bookings, unresolved disputes, incomplete payments, outstanding balances, or other matters requiring your attention.

You should also download or retain any invoices, receipts, booking confirmations, or other records that you may need in the future, as these may no longer be accessible after your account has been deleted.

If you have linked your Saral Pooja account with any third-party service, you may also wish to review the privacy settings and permissions associated with those services.

12. Security and Fraud Prevention

Saral Pooja reserves the right to delay or decline an account deletion request where it reasonably believes that the request is fraudulent, unauthorized, intended to evade legal obligations, or connected to ongoing investigations relating to fraud, misuse of the Platform, payment disputes, criminal activity, or violations of applicable law.

In such situations, only the minimum amount of information necessary for investigation, legal compliance, or the protection of our legitimate interests will be retained.

13. Children's Accounts

Saral Pooja is intended for individuals who are legally capable of entering into a binding agreement under applicable law.

If we become aware that an account has been created or used in violation of applicable age requirements, we may suspend or delete the account and take any other action considered appropriate under our policies and applicable law.

Parents or legal guardians who believe that a child has created an account without appropriate authorization may contact our customer support team to request review and appropriate action.

14. Changes to this Account Deletion Policy

Saral Pooja may update this Account Deletion Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our Services, legal obligations, technology, operational practices, or business requirements.

Whenever material changes are made, the updated version will be published on the Platform with a revised "Last Updated" date. Where required by law, we may also notify users through email, SMS, push notifications, or other appropriate communication channels.

Your continued use of the Platform after such updates become effective constitutes your acceptance of the revised Policy to the extent permitted by applicable law.

By using the Saral Pooja Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Account Deletion Policy and agree to the procedures described herein regarding the deletion of your account and associated information.
