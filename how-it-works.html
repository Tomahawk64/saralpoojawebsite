How It Works

Simple. Convenient. Spiritual.

Booking a religious or spiritual service through Saral Pooja follows four steps, from choosing a service to receiving confirmation.

Choose a service

Browse the available pujas, rituals, temple services or other spiritual services on the platform.

Select your requirements

Choose your preferred date, time, location and any other details required for the booking.

Book & confirm

Submit your booking request and complete payment, where applicable.

Get your service

Saral Pooja coordinates your booking with the assigned, available verified pandit or service provider.

About booking confirmation

Submitting a booking request does not automatically guarantee confirmation. A booking is confirmed once the following are in place:

  • Availability of the selected service
  • Successful payment, where applicable
  • Confirmation from Saral Pooja or the assigned service provider

How you'll hear from us

Booking confirmations and updates may be communicated through:

  • The Saral Pooja platform
  • SMS
  • Email
  • WhatsApp
  • Other available communication channels

If a service cannot be provided as scheduled, Saral Pooja will make reasonable efforts to offer an alternative time, assign another qualified service provider, or process a refund in accordance with our Refund Policy. Read the full booking and cancellation terms in our Terms & Conditions.
