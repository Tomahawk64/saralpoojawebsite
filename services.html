For your home & family

Home & family poojas

Ceremonies for new beginnings, prosperity, health and peace — performed at your home, flat or office.

Ganesh Pooja Seek the blessings of Lord Ganesha for a new beginning. ▾

Ganesh Pooja invokes Lord Ganesha, the remover of obstacles, before any important undertaking — a new home, a new venture, a wedding or a festival. It is one of the most commonly requested poojas for a smooth, auspicious start.

Commonly performed for

  • Removing obstacles
  • Success in new beginnings
  • Prosperity & growth
  • Peace of mind

Satyanarayana Katha Seek Lord Vishnu's blessings for peace, prosperity and well-being. ▾

The Satyanarayana Katha is a devotional narration and pooja dedicated to Lord Vishnu, traditionally performed on Purnima, after a wish is fulfilled, or on any auspicious family occasion. The katha is recited as per Vedic vidhi and Puranokta niyam, followed by aarti and prasad.

Commonly performed for

  • Peace & harmony at home
  • Prosperity for the family
  • Gratitude after a wish fulfilled
  • Housewarmings & special occasions
Book in the app How booking works
Ideal for homes, offices and special occasions. Subject to location, date and pandit availability.
Griha Pravesh Pooja Shubh aarambh — invite positivity and blessings into your new home. ▾

Griha Pravesh is the traditional housewarming ceremony performed before you move into a new home, flat or office. It typically includes a Kalash sthapana, Ganesh and Navgraha pooja, a Vastu shanti havan and a full purnahuti, done as per Vedic vidhi.

Commonly performed for

  • An auspicious start in a new home
  • Positive energy & Vastu shanti
  • Prosperity & well-being
  • Removing negativity
Book in the app How booking works
For new homes, flats and offices. Post-pooja samagri is collected and responsibly recycled. Subject to location, date and pandit availability.
Office Inauguration Pooja Shubh aarambh, safal bhavishya — blessings for your new workspace. ▾

An inauguration pooja for a new office, shop or workplace, seeking divine blessings for growth, prosperity and success. It usually includes Ganesh pooja, Lakshmi pooja and a havan, with an option to invite blessings for the team and the space.

Commonly performed for

  • An auspicious start for a new venture
  • Success & prosperity in business
  • Positive energy for growth
  • Blessings for the team & workplace

Rudrabhishek Invoke the infinite grace of Lord Shiva. ▾

Rudrabhishek is the ceremonial abhishek of the Shivling with water, milk, honey, curd and other offerings, accompanied by chanting of the Rudram. It is performed to seek the blessings of Lord Shiva for peace of mind, good health and the fulfilment of sincere desires.

Commonly performed for

  • Removal of obstacles & doshas
  • Good health & longevity
  • Prosperity
  • Peace of mind

Sundarkand Path Invite strength, protection and positive energy into your life. ▾

Sundarkand Path is a complete recitation of the Sundarkand chapter of the Ramcharitmanas, performed with devotion and faith. It is a popular seva for the whole family, often held at home on Tuesdays or Saturdays and followed by aarti and prasad.

Commonly performed for

  • Courage & inner strength
  • Protection & removal of negativity
  • Peace & harmony in the family
  • Devotion & well-being

Performed as per Vedic vidhi and Puranokta niyam. Ideal for homes, offices and special occasions.
Lakshmi Kubera Maha Pooja Invoke wealth, prosperity and endless abundance. ▾

The Lakshmi Kubera Maha Pooja is performed together to Goddess Lakshmi and Lord Kubera, seeking financial stability, business success and lasting abundance. It is commonly requested on Fridays, Diwali, Akshaya Tritiya and at the start of a new financial venture.

Commonly performed for

  • Wealth growth
  • Financial stability
  • Business success
  • Abundance in life

Mahamrityunjaya Jaap For health, protection and long life. ▾

The Mahamrityunjaya Jaap is a dedicated chanting of the Mahamrityunjaya Mantra, often in a specified count, followed by a havan. It is traditionally undertaken during illness, recovery, difficult planetary periods or for the general well-being of a family member.

Commonly performed for

  • Good health & recovery
  • Protection from harm
  • Relief during difficult times
  • Peace of mind & long life

Pitra Dosh Shanti Pooja Seek the blessings of ancestors for a peaceful life. ▾

Pitra Dosh Shanti Pooja is performed to honour ancestors and address difficulties attributed to Pitra Dosh in the horoscope. It typically includes tarpan, shraddh vidhi, daan and a shanti havan, often on Amavasya or during Pitru Paksha.

Commonly performed for

  • Ancestral blessings
  • Removal of obstacles
  • Peace & stability
  • Happiness in family life

Grah shanti & devi sadhana

Dosh nivaran, grah shanti & sadhana

Poojas and jaaps to balance planetary influences and remove doshas, plus devi sadhanas — many available as guided online poojas with verified pandits.

Navgraha Shanti Pooja Harmonise the nine planets for a brighter future. ▾

Navgraha Shanti Pooja invokes the nine planetary deities (Surya, Chandra, Mangal, Budh, Guru, Shukra, Shani, Rahu and Ketu) to reduce malefic planetary effects and bring balance. It is commonly done before a major event or during an unfavourable dasha.

Commonly performed for

  • Balancing planetary effects
  • Prosperity & progress
  • Good health & well-being
  • Peace of mind

Kaal Sarp Dosh Nivaran Pooja Remove obstacles, bring balance and new beginnings. ▾

Shani Dev Shanti Pooja Peace, justice and relief from Shani dosha. ▾

Shani Dev Shanti Pooja is undertaken during Sade Sati, Dhaiya or an unfavourable Shani period, seeking relief and steadiness. It usually includes Shani jaap, tel abhishek, black til and udad offerings, and daan, performed on a Saturday.

Commonly performed for

  • Relief during Sade Sati / Dhaiya
  • Steadiness in work & life
  • Reducing delays & setbacks
  • Peace of mind

A note on availability

Whether a particular pooja can be provided at your preferred time and place can depend on a number of factors, including:

  • Your location
  • Date and time
  • Verified pandit availability
  • Festival schedules
  • Weather
  • Public holidays
  • Travel restrictions
  • Government restrictions
  • Other operational circumstances

If a service cannot be provided as scheduled, Saral Pooja may attempt to offer another time, assign another suitable provider, provide an appropriate alternative, or process a refund in accordance with our Refund Policy.
