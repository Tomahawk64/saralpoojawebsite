About Saral Pooja

Bringing puja & spiritual services closer to you

Saral Pooja is a digital platform that makes it easier to discover, book, coordinate and manage religious and spiritual services by connecting you with verified pandits and religious service providers.

Our purpose

The platform is designed around making traditional religious services more convenient, accessible and organised through technology. Saral Pooja connects users with verified pandits and service providers for pujas, rituals, temple services, spiritual consultations and other devotional services.

From home pujas and temple services to spiritual consultations and devotional offerings, Saral Pooja helps you organise your spiritual needs conveniently — without losing the trust and tradition these services are built on.

🕉️

Traditional, made convenient

We combine Indian spiritual practice with a modern, trustworthy marketplace — so booking a puja feels as simple as it should.

What we help you do

Saral Pooja brings together the people and services involved in religious and spiritual life, in one place.

🛕

Discover

Find temples, verified pandits and services near you, based on location and availability.

📅

Book

Choose your requirements — date, time, location — and submit a booking request.

🤝

Coordinate

We help coordinate confirmation, communication and support around your booking.

An important note

Religious services provided through Saral Pooja are based on traditional customs and the practices of individual service providers. Religious practices may vary by region, tradition, temple and pandit. Saral Pooja does not guarantee any specific spiritual, religious, emotional, financial, medical or supernatural outcome.

Have a question for us?

Our support team is happy to help with bookings, services or general questions about the platform.
